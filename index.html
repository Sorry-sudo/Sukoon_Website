FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our guests.

We're located in Gayool, approximately 10 minutes by car from Skardu city center. We offer complimentary pickup service from Skardu bus stand and airport - please inform us of your arrival details at least 24 hours in advance. Taxis and local transport are also readily available.

We provide free airport/bus station pickup and drop-off for all guests. For local sightseeing tours (Shangrila Resort, Kachura Lake, Deosai Plains, etc.), we can arrange private vehicles at additional cost. Our team will help plan your entire itinerary.

We accept both cash (PKR) and card payments. Major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) are accepted with a 2% processing fee. Bank transfer is also available for advance payments. Please note that card machines may occasionally be unavailable due to connectivity issues - we recommend carrying sufficient cash as backup.

We provide complimentary Wi-Fi in all rooms and common areas. Internet speed in Skardu can vary depending on weather and network conditions. Our connection is suitable for browsing, social media, and video calls, though speeds may be slower than in major cities. We recommend downloading essential files before your trip.

最佳季节: May to October offers pleasant weather (15-25°C) with clear mountain views. Summer (Jun-Aug): Warm days, perfect for trekking and lake visits. Autumn (Sep-Oct): Stunning fall colors and harvest season. Winter (Nov-Feb): Snow-covered landscapes and skiing opportunities, though some routes may close. Spring (Mar-Apr): Cherry/apricot blossoms - photographer's paradise!

We're perfectly located near major attractions: Shangrila Resort (5 min), Satpara Dam (15 min), Kachura Lake (45 min), Shigar Fort (40 min), and Khaplu Palace (2 hrs). Day trips to Deosai Plains (world's second-highest plateau) and Cold Desert are highly recommended. We'll help you plan the perfect itinerary!

Nearby Attractions

Shangrila Resort

Famous "Heaven on Earth" resort with Lower Kachura Lake views and historic aircraft restaurant.

Kachura Lake

Crystal-clear alpine lake surrounded by snow-capped peaks. Ideal for boating and picnics.

2 hrs drive

Deosai Plains

World's second-highest plateau at 4,114m. Home to Tibetan bears and wildflowers (Jun-Aug).
